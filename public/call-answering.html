FAQ

Call Answering Questions

Who actually answers my calls?
A real UK based person from your dedicated Multicore team. No overseas call centre, no robots and no scripts. The same people handle your line day in day out, so they get to know your business and your regulars.
Do callers know it's not me answering?
We answer in your business name, so to the caller it's simply your office picking up. Most of our clients find customers are impressed they've got proper office support.
What happens with urgent calls?
You tell us what counts as urgent, a burst pipe, a power cut, a customer you've been waiting on and we flag those to you straight away by text or WhatsApp. Everything else is in your end of day summary.
Can I keep my existing phone number?
Yes. You keep your number and simply divert it to us whenever you want cover, all day, just when you're on the tools or only when you don't pick up. You stay in control.
How much does call answering cost?
Call answering is included in every Multicore package alongside quoting, email and diary management. Pricing is tailored to the size of your business, so give us a ring on 020 8049 5144 for a quick no pressure chat.
What hours do you cover?
Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 5pm. Outside those hours callers get a professional voicemail and anything left overnight is actioned first thing.
